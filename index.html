🎓 AutoScribe Autonomous Voice Exam Portal

Designed for 100% independent exam writing by blind students. Zero visual dependency. Simply listen to audio prompts and control the entire portal by speaking.

🎙️ Continuous Hands-Free Listening Active (Say "Login" or "Start Exam")

🗣️ Core Navigation Voice Commands

"Login"
Opens Student Voice Login Screen
"Start Exam"
Directly launches active examination session
"Read question"
Reads question text aloud via Text-to-Speech
"Start answer"
Activates continuous voice dictation
"Read my answer"
Verifies transcribed text by reading back answer
"Submit exam"
Triggers secure submission with voice confirmation
